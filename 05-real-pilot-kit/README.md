# Real Pilot Kit — get real users in 3 weeks

This answers the other two manager questions:
- **"Did anyone actually use it?"** → a real business uses your automation for 3 weeks
- **"Can I trust this person?"** → you planned it, measured it, handed it over, and the owner writes you a reference

## The plan

| Week | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0 | Ask 5–10 small businesses (café, hostel, small hotel, shop, Fahrschule, sports club). Use `01_anschreiben.md`. Going in person works best. |
| 0 | One says yes → agree on the 1-page `02_pilot_vereinbarung.md` |
| 1 | **Measure first**: how long the task takes *today* (`03_messung.csv`). Without this you have no result. |
| 1 | Set up the automation (review replies or bookings, see below) |
| 2–3 | It runs. Check in once a week, fix small things, keep measuring |
| 3 | Hand over with `04_uebergabe_anleitung.md`, ask for a reference (`05_referenz_anfrage.md`) |

## What to offer (pick one, keep it small)

**A. Review reply assistant** (from the n8n project)
New Google review → AI writes a reply draft in German or English → owner gets it by email or WhatsApp, edits and posts it. Weekly summary of what guests say.
Good for: cafés, restaurants, hostels, small hotels.
*Note:* reading Google reviews automatically needs the owner's Google Business Profile access. The simple version: the owner forwards the review email notification to an address the workflow reads. Start simple.

**B. Booking / sign-up handling** (from the Zapier project)
Online form → confirmation email → calendar entry → reminder the day before.
Good for: Nachhilfe, Fahrschule, small studios, clubs, events.

## Rules that build trust
- **Free, small and short.** 3 weeks, one task. Don't promise more.
- **Their data stays theirs.** Use their own Google account where possible. No customer data on your laptop. Only touch what you need.
- **AI only drafts, people decide.** Nothing goes to customers without the owner seeing it first.
- **Measure honestly.** If it saved 40 minutes a week, write 40 minutes, not "hours".
- **Leave it working.** Even if they stop using it, they should be able to turn it off without you.
