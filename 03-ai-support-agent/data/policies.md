# Rentora Rental Policies (fictional demo company)

## Deposit
A refundable deposit is blocked on the customer's credit card at pickup: 300 EUR for Economy and Compact, 500 EUR for SUV and Van, 1000 EUR for Premium. The block is released within 7 business days after return if the car has no new damage. Debit cards are accepted for Economy and Compact only.
Stichwörter: Kaution, Sicherheitsleistung, Kreditkarte, Debitkarte, Freigabe

## Cancellation
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup. Cancellations between 48 and 24 hours before pickup cost 25 EUR. Later cancellations or no-shows are charged one rental day. Prepaid "Saver" rates are non-refundable.
Stichwörter: Stornierung, stornieren, cancel, free, kostenlos, No-Show, Erstattung

## Fuel
Cars are handed over with a full tank and must be returned full. If not, Rentora refuels at 2.50 EUR per litre plus a 15 EUR service fee. Electric cars must be returned with at least 70 percent charge.
Stichwörter: Tank, tanken, vollgetankt, volltanken, Benzin, Kraftstoff, Laden, Elektroauto, Akku

## Young drivers
Minimum age is 19 with at least one year of driving licence. Drivers under 25 pay a young driver fee of 12 EUR per day and cannot rent Premium cars.
Stichwörter: Mindestalter, junge Fahrer, Führerschein, Alter, Zuschlag

## Additional driver
An additional driver costs 8 EUR per day and must be present at pickup with a valid licence.
Stichwörter: Zusatzfahrer, zweiter Fahrer, weiterer Fahrer

## Cross-border travel
Travel to EU countries, Switzerland, Norway and the UK is allowed. Travel to other countries requires written approval. Premium cars may not leave Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
Stichwörter: Ausland, Grenze, Europa, Österreich, Schweiz, Auslandsfahrt

## Damage and accidents
In case of an accident, call the police and the Rentora 24/7 hotline at +49 89 000000. Do not admit liability. Damage is covered by the included insurance with an excess of 950 EUR, or 0 EUR with the Zero Excess package.
Stichwörter: Unfall, Schaden, Versicherung, Selbstbeteiligung, Kratzer, Delle

## Late return
There is a 59 minute grace period. After that, each started hour costs 15 EUR, up to the price of one rental day.
Stichwörter: Verspätung, späte Rückgabe, zu spät, Rückgabe

## Mileage
Economy and Compact include 300 km per day. SUV, Van and Premium include 250 km per day. Each extra kilometre costs 0.29 EUR.
Stichwörter: Kilometer, Freikilometer, km, Mehrkilometer
